About SaniCore

More Than a Gym — A Lifestyle

Founded in 2013, SaniCore Fitness Center has grown from a local gym into a full-scale wellness destination. We believe fitness is not a phase — it's a way of life.

Our facility spans over 12,000 sq ft of premium training space, featuring the latest cardio machines, free weights, functional training zones, and dedicated class studios.

Whether you're taking your first step into fitness or training for competition, our expert team is here to guide, challenge, and inspire you every day.
