Our Story

More Than Iron & Sweat

SaniCore was born from a simple belief: that every person in Negombo deserves world-class fitness support — not just those who already look the part.

Founded in 2013 with a modest 3,000 sq ft space and a handful of barbells, we've grown into a 12,000 sq ft training facility that Negombo is proud to call its own. What hasn't changed is our obsession with results and people.

Over 5,000 members have walked through our doors. We've watched beginners become athletes, athletes become champions, and everyone in between rediscover confidence they didn't know they had.

Today, SaniCore stands as Sri Lanka's most trusted fitness destination — recognised for expert coaching, premium equipment, and a culture that genuinely lifts people up.
